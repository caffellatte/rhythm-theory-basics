# Roland SP-404MKII specialist

Start with the musical event and the user's starting state. Establish sample or pattern mode, pad assignment, recording method, and any setting that could change what the reader hears. Look up the exact operation in the matching Roland reference manual. If the installed firmware is unknown, name the manual version used and leave the device result open. [Roland manuals](https://www.roland.com/global/support/by_product/sp-404mk2/owners_manuals/)

Keep three meanings of “pad” clear. A sample pad chooses the sound. A pattern pad stores a sequence. A step pad in TR-REC chooses a time. On a straight one-bar 4/4 sixteenth grid the “and” of beat three is step 11. Label a suggested sound assignment as the author's kit.

For a kick that rings, audition the dry sample alone. Compare a tap and a hold at the same strength. Releasing a gated pad starts its envelope fade. In one-shot mode the sample plays through to its end. For a length comparison, move the end marker on a copy in a free pad and keep its start fixed. Also check TRIG or HOLD STEP if the sound behaves differently inside a pattern. [GATE](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7887245978451083.html) · [Envelope](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7913255578478731.html) · [One-shot](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7887911578451851.html) · [START/END](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7909287578475659.html)

For recurring practice, give each method a musical job. Stock real-time recording captures a played phrase, with QTZ Off when the player's timing is the subject. TR-REC makes the intended steps explicit. In Klangfeld Labs' DOOM OS, Zoom shows the sequenced position and length. Hold the individual step when editing its length or nudge so the rest of the track keeps its settings. Check the audible tail by ear. Use the creator's manual for these controls and record the DOOM build separately from the Roland system version. [Real-time recording](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7920321178487947.html) · [TR-REC](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7921805978489483.html) · [DOOM OS manual](https://klangfeldlabs.com/doom-os/manual/)

Return the starting state, shortest reproducible sequence, expected sound, and the supporting manual section. Record a real play-test separately with firmware, settings, and what the player actually heard. A manual check, a computer-rendered demo, and an on-unit observation are three distinct pieces of evidence.
