Answer key. In A the short kicks land on one and three. In B the second kick moved to three-and, where it meets the bass. In C it rang longer on three, for 500 ms against A's 125 ms. The bass enters about 333 ms after beat three, under the ringing kick.

Live pads. Tap your foot on the four beats. Voice a short dum on three, then try it on the following “and.” Return to three and stretch the mmm across the bass entrance. In C major, sing do with the bass and sol with the answering melodic sample. Record a live pass with QTZ Off and listen before looking at the grid. Roland real-time recording

TR-REC. Press PATTERN SELECT, then REC, and choose a blinking empty pattern pad. Use REMAIN to select TR-REC. Set 90 BPM, one bar of 4/4, and zero shuffle. Press REC to begin. Hold SUB PAD and tap the sample pad, then release SUB PAD and enter the time steps in TRIG mode. For A, place short kicks on 1 and 9. For B move 9 to 11. For C use the short kick on 1 and only its longer copy on 9. Keep the bass on 3 and 11 throughout. To make a short copy, keep its start fixed and bring its end marker earlier. TR-REC · START/END

DOOM OS. In a practice pattern select the kick track and inspect steps 9 and 11 in the graphical grid. Predict which kick meets the bass, then switch to Zoom and listen. On a gated sound, hold the chosen step and turn CTRL 2 to experiment with its sequenced length. A one-shot can play to its end marker, and a gated release can fade after the note ends.
